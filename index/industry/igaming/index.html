iGaming Industry

The global iGaming industry encompasses online casinos, sportsbooks, poker, and sweepstakes platforms. US market leaders BetMGM, DraftKings, and FanDuel are targeting a $55-$80 billion opportunity by 2030.

Key Concepts

Real Money Gaming (RMG)

Licensed online gambling where players wager and win real currency.

Sweepstakes Model

Legal US gaming model using dual-currency (Gold Coins + Sweeps Coins) to operate in states without RMG licenses.

GGR

Gross Gaming Revenue: total wagers minus winnings paid to players. The primary revenue metric for operators.

KYC / AML

Know Your Customer and Anti-Money Laundering compliance requirements for all licensed operators.

Responsible Gambling

Operator obligations to detect and protect problem gamblers, including self-exclusion tools and deposit limits.
