State Legal Baseline Overview

In Michigan, commercial advertising and consumer protection are governed generally by Michigan Attorney General under Michigan Consumer Protection Act (MCL 445.901). Gambling activities and licensed promotional rules are separately regulated by Michigan Gaming Control Board (MGCB). Commercial online real-money casinos and mobile sports betting are fully legal and regulated under MCL 432.301 / MCL 432.401.

Consumer Protection & Enforcement

Consumer protection in Michigan is enforced by the Michigan Attorney General under Michigan Consumer Protection Act (MCL 445.901), requiring clear pricing disclosures, non-deceptive terms, and truthful marketing. Specific gambling disclosures are governed under Michigan Gaming Control Board (MGCB) regulations.
