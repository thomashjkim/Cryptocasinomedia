4. Advertising & Consumer Protection Standards

Digital advertising and promotional publishing targeting audiences in Portland-Auburn, Maine are subject to general consumer protection standards under Maine Unfair Trade Practices Act (5 M.R.S.A. § 205-A) (prohibiting false or deceptive marketing) as well as FTC endorsement principles requiring clear disclosure of commercial or affiliate relationships. Where authorized, licensed gambling advertising must comply with statutory responsible gaming disclosure rules enforced by Maine Gambling Control Unit (MGCU).

General commercial advertising in Portland-Auburn is subject to truth-in-advertising standards under Maine Unfair Trade Practices Act (5 M.R.S.A. § 205-A).. Covered gambling promotions are separately subject to statutory responsible gaming disclosures under state gaming regulations (Maine Gambling Control Unit (MGCU)).

5. Sweepstakes, Social Casino & Prediction Markets
