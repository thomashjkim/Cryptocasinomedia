State Legal Baseline Overview

In Massachusetts, commercial advertising and consumer protection are governed generally by Massachusetts Attorney General under Massachusetts Consumer Protection Act (M.G.L. c. 93A). Gambling activities and licensed promotional rules are separately regulated by Massachusetts Gaming Commission (MGC). Sports wagering (mobile and retail) is legal under M.G.L. c. 23K / c. 23N. Online real-money casinos remain illegal.

Consumer Protection & Enforcement

Consumer protection in Massachusetts is enforced by the Massachusetts Attorney General under Massachusetts Consumer Protection Act (M.G.L. c. 93A), requiring clear pricing disclosures, non-deceptive terms, and truthful marketing. Specific gambling disclosures are governed under Massachusetts Gaming Commission (MGC) regulations.
