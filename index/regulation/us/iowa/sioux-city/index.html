The Sioux City DMA spans multiple state boundaries. Below are the distinct state-level regulatory standards applicable depending on physical location of activity:

Iowa Regulatory Framework US-IA

Gaming Status: Mobile sports wagering is legal under Iowa Code Chapter 99F. Online casino gambling is prohibited.

Consumer Protection: Iowa Attorney General (Iowa Consumer Fraud Act (Iowa Code § 714.16))

Gaming Authority: Iowa Racing and Gaming Commission (IRGC)

Nebraska Regulatory Framework US-NE

Gaming Status: Retail sports wagering at licensed horse tracks is authorized under Neb. Rev. Stat. § 9-1101. Mobile online wagering is prohibited.

Consumer Protection: Nebraska Attorney General (Nebraska Consumer Protection Act (Neb. Rev. Stat. § 59-1601))

Gaming Authority: Nebraska Gaming and Parks Commission & Racing and Gaming Commission

South Dakota Regulatory Framework US-SD

Gaming Status: Sports wagering is legal strictly in-person within Deadwood commercial casinos (SDCL § 42-7B-1). Online mobile casino gambling is prohibited.

Consumer Protection: South Dakota Attorney General (South Dakota Deceptive Trade Practices Act (SDCL § 37-24-6))

Gaming Authority: South Dakota Commission on Gaming (SDCG)

2. Gambling, iGaming & Sports Betting Status

Mobile sports wagering is legal under Iowa Code Chapter 99F. Online casino gambling is prohibited.

Commercial Online Casino: Not authorized in Iowa. Iowa does not license commercial online casino operators.
Sports Betting Status: Authorized and regulated by Iowa Racing and Gaming Commission (IRGC).
Primary Gaming Regulator: Iowa Racing and Gaming Commission (IRGC)

3. Crypto Gambling & Digital Asset Regulations

Money transmitters regulated under Iowa Code Chapter 533C by the Division of Banking.

Financial institutions and digital asset transmitters facilitating transactions within Sioux City are subject to US-IA money transmitter statutes and regulatory licensing.

4. Advertising & Consumer Protection Standards

Digital advertising and promotional publishing targeting audiences in Sioux City, Iowa are subject to general consumer protection standards under Iowa Consumer Fraud Act (Iowa Code § 714.16) (prohibiting false or deceptive marketing) as well as FTC endorsement principles requiring clear disclosure of commercial or affiliate relationships. Where authorized, licensed gambling advertising must comply with statutory responsible gaming disclosure rules enforced by Iowa Racing and Gaming Commission (IRGC).

General commercial advertising in Sioux City is subject to truth-in-advertising standards under Iowa Consumer Fraud Act (Iowa Code § 714.16).. Covered gambling promotions are separately subject to statutory responsible gaming disclosures under state gaming regulations (Iowa Racing and Gaming Commission (IRGC)).

5. Sweepstakes, Social Casino & Prediction Markets

Promotional sweepstakes and social gaming in Iowa are subject to state promotional contest rules and consumer protection standards under Iowa Consumer Fraud Act (Iowa Code § 714.16).

Dual-Currency & Simulated Gambling: Subject to Iowa consumer protection statutes (Iowa Consumer Fraud Act (Iowa Code § 714.16)) and statutory gambling definitions.
Statutory Code: Iowa Consumer Fraud Act (Iowa Code § 714.16)
Prediction Markets: Event-contract prediction markets may fall under CFTC federal oversight, while state gambling and consumer-protection laws can also create separate legal or enforcement issues.
