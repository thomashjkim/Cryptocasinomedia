2. Gambling, iGaming & Sports Betting Status

Commercial online real-money casinos and mobile sports betting are prohibited under N.D.C.C. § 12.1-28-01.

Commercial Online Casino: Not authorized in North Dakota. North Dakota does not license commercial online casino operators.
Sports Betting Status: Authorized and regulated by North Dakota Attorney General Gaming Division.
Primary Gaming Regulator: North Dakota Attorney General Gaming Division

3. Crypto Gambling & Digital Asset Regulations

Crypto transmitters regulated under N.D.C.C. Ch. 13-09.1 by Department of Financial Institutions.

Financial institutions and digital asset transmitters facilitating transactions within Fargo-Valley City are subject to US-ND money transmitter statutes and regulatory licensing.

4. Advertising & Consumer Protection Standards

Digital advertising and promotional publishing targeting audiences in Fargo-Valley City, North Dakota are subject to general consumer protection standards under North Dakota Unlawful Sales Practices Act (N.D.C.C. § 51-15-02) (prohibiting false or deceptive marketing) as well as FTC endorsement principles requiring clear disclosure of commercial or affiliate relationships. Where authorized, licensed gambling advertising must comply with statutory responsible gaming disclosure rules enforced by North Dakota Attorney General Gaming Division.

General commercial advertising in Fargo-Valley City is subject to truth-in-advertising standards under North Dakota Unlawful Sales Practices Act (N.D.C.C. § 51-15-02).. Covered gambling promotions are separately subject to statutory responsible gaming disclosures under state gaming regulations (North Dakota Attorney General Gaming Division).

5. Sweepstakes, Social Casino & Prediction Markets

Promotional sweepstakes and social gaming in North Dakota are subject to state promotional contest rules and consumer protection standards under North Dakota Unlawful Sales Practices Act (N.D.C.C. § 51-15-02).

Dual-Currency & Simulated Gambling: Subject to North Dakota consumer protection statutes (North Dakota Unlawful Sales Practices Act (N.D.C.C. § 51-15-02)) and statutory gambling definitions.
Statutory Code: North Dakota Unlawful Sales Practices Act (N.D.C.C. § 51-15-02)
Prediction Markets: Event-contract prediction markets may fall under CFTC federal oversight, while state gambling and consumer-protection laws can also create separate legal or enforcement issues.
