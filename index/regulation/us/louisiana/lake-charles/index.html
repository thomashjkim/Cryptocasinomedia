4. Advertising & Consumer Protection Standards

Digital advertising and promotional publishing targeting audiences in Lake Charles, Louisiana are subject to general consumer protection standards under Louisiana Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law (LUTPA) (La. R.S. 51:1401) (prohibiting false or deceptive marketing) as well as FTC endorsement principles requiring clear disclosure of commercial or affiliate relationships. Where authorized, licensed gambling advertising must comply with statutory responsible gaming disclosure rules enforced by Louisiana Gaming Control Board (LGCB).

General commercial advertising in Lake Charles is subject to truth-in-advertising standards under Louisiana Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law (LUTPA) (La. R.S. 51:1401).. Covered gambling promotions are separately subject to statutory responsible gaming disclosures under state gaming regulations (Louisiana Gaming Control Board (LGCB)).

5. Sweepstakes, Social Casino & Prediction Markets

Promotional sweepstakes and social gaming in Louisiana are subject to state promotional contest rules and consumer protection standards under Louisiana Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law (LUTPA) (La. R.S. 51:1401).

Dual-Currency & Simulated Gambling: Subject to Louisiana consumer protection statutes (Louisiana Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law (LUTPA) (La. R.S. 51:1401)) and statutory gambling definitions.
