State Legal Baseline Overview

In Alabama, commercial advertising and consumer protection are governed generally by Alabama Attorney General under Alabama Deceptive Trade Practices Act (Alabama Code § 8-19-1 et seq.). Gambling activities and licensed promotional rules are separately regulated by Alabama Electronic Gambling Task Force & State Licensing Authorities. Commercial online casinos and online sports betting remain illegal under Alabama Code § 13A-12-20. Tribal gaming is operated under federal IGRA by the Poarch Band of Creek Indians.

Consumer Protection & Enforcement

Consumer protection in Alabama is enforced by the Alabama Attorney General under Alabama Deceptive Trade Practices Act (Alabama Code § 8-19-1 et seq.), requiring clear pricing disclosures, non-deceptive terms, and truthful marketing. Specific gambling disclosures are governed under Alabama Electronic Gambling Task Force & State Licensing Authorities regulations.
