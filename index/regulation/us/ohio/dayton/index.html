1. Dayton Regulatory Overview

The Dayton Nielsen DMA operates under the statutory regulatory framework of Ohio. General advertising and consumer protection standards are enforced by the Ohio Attorney General under Ohio Consumer Sales Practices Act (CSPA) (ORC 1345.01 et seq.)..

2. Gambling, iGaming & Sports Betting Status

Sports gaming (mobile and retail) is legal under ORC Chapter 3775. Online real-money casino gambling is prohibited.

Commercial Online Casino: Not authorized in Ohio. Ohio does not license commercial online casino operators.
Sports Betting Status: Not authorized in Ohio. Ohio does not currently license commercial mobile sports betting operators.

4. Advertising & Consumer Protection Standards

Digital advertising and promotional publishing targeting audiences in Dayton, Ohio are subject to general consumer protection standards under Ohio Consumer Sales Practices Act (CSPA) (ORC 1345.01 et seq.) (prohibiting false or deceptive marketing) as well as FTC endorsement principles requiring clear disclosure of commercial or affiliate relationships. Where authorized, licensed gambling advertising must comply with statutory responsible gaming disclosure rules enforced by Ohio Casino Control Commission (OCCC) & Ohio Lottery.

General commercial advertising in Dayton is subject to truth-in-advertising standards under Ohio Consumer Sales Practices Act (CSPA) (ORC 1345.01 et seq.).. Covered gambling promotions are separately subject to statutory responsible gaming disclosures under state gaming regulations (Ohio Casino Control Commission (OCCC) & Ohio Lottery).

5. Sweepstakes, Social Casino & Prediction Markets

Promotional sweepstakes and social gaming in Ohio are subject to state promotional contest rules and consumer protection standards under Ohio Consumer Sales Practices Act (CSPA) (ORC 1345.01 et seq.).

Dual-Currency & Simulated Gambling: Subject to Ohio consumer protection statutes (Ohio Consumer Sales Practices Act (CSPA) (ORC 1345.01 et seq.)) and statutory gambling definitions.
Statutory Code: Ohio Consumer Sales Practices Act (CSPA) (ORC 1345.01 et seq.)
Prediction Markets: Event-contract prediction markets may fall under CFTC federal oversight, while state gambling and consumer-protection laws can also create separate legal or enforcement issues.

6. Municipal & DMA Local Context

No local municipal ordinances in Dayton materially alter Ohio state-level legal and regulatory standards. State law applies across all municipalities within this market.
