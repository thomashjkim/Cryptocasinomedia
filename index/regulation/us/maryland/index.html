State Legal Baseline Overview

In Maryland, commercial advertising and consumer protection are governed generally by Maryland Attorney General under Maryland Consumer Protection Act (Md. Code Ann., Com. Law § 13-101). Gambling activities and licensed promotional rules are separately regulated by Maryland State Lottery and Gaming Control Agency (MSLGCA). Mobile sports wagering is legal under Md. Code Ann., State Gov't § 9-1E-01. Online real-money casino games are unauthorized.

Consumer Protection & Enforcement

Consumer protection in Maryland is enforced by the Maryland Attorney General under Maryland Consumer Protection Act (Md. Code Ann., Com. Law § 13-101), requiring clear pricing disclosures, non-deceptive terms, and truthful marketing. Specific gambling disclosures are governed under Maryland State Lottery and Gaming Control Agency (MSLGCA) regulations.
