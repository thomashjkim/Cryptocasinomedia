The Washington DC-Hagerstown DMA spans multiple state boundaries. Below are the distinct state-level regulatory standards applicable depending on physical location of activity:

District of Columbia Regulatory Framework US-DC

Gaming Status: Mobile sports wagering is legal under DC Code § 36-621.01 via licensed platforms. Commercial online real-money casino games remain unauthorized.

Consumer Protection: District of Columbia Office of the Attorney General (OAG) (District of Columbia Consumer Protection Procedures Act (DC Code § 28-3901 et seq.))

Maryland Regulatory Framework US-MD

Gaming Status: Mobile sports wagering is legal under Md. Code Ann., State Gov't § 9-1E-01. Online real-money casino games are unauthorized.

Consumer Protection: Maryland Attorney General (Maryland Consumer Protection Act (Md. Code Ann., Com. Law § 13-101))

Gaming Authority: Maryland State Lottery and Gaming Control Agency (MSLGCA)

Virginia Regulatory Framework US-VA

Gaming Status: Sports betting (mobile and retail) is legal under Va. Code § 58.1-4030. Online real-money casino games remain unauthorized.

Consumer Protection: Virginia Attorney General (Virginia Consumer Protection Act (VCPA) (Va. Code § 59.1-196))

Gaming Authority: Virginia Lottery Gaming Division

West Virginia Regulatory Framework US-WV

Gaming Status: Online casino gambling and sports betting are fully legal and regulated under W. Va. Code § 29-22D / § 29-22E.

Consumer Protection: West Virginia Attorney General (West Virginia Consumer Credit and Protection Act (WVCCPA) (W. Va. Code § 46A-6-101))

Gaming Authority: West Virginia Lottery Commission

2. Gambling, iGaming & Sports Betting Status

Mobile sports wagering is legal under DC Code § 36-621.01 via licensed platforms. Commercial online real-money casino games remain unauthorized.

Commercial Online Casino: Commercial online real-money casinos are not authorized in the District of Columbia.
Sports Betting Status: Online sports betting is authorized and regulated under DC Code § 36-621.01.
Primary Gaming Regulator: District of Columbia Office of Lottery and Gaming (OLG)

3. Crypto Gambling & Digital Asset Regulations

Money transmitters dealing in digital assets are licensed under the DC Transmitters of Money Act (DC Code § 26-1001) by DISB.

Financial institutions and digital asset transmitters facilitating transactions within Washington DC-Hagerstown are subject to US-DC money transmitter statutes and regulatory licensing.

4. Advertising & Consumer Protection Standards

Digital advertising and promotional publishing targeting audiences in Washington DC-Hagerstown, District of Columbia are subject to general consumer protection standards under District of Columbia Consumer Protection Procedures Act (DC Code § 28-3901 et seq.) (prohibiting false or deceptive marketing) as well as FTC endorsement principles requiring clear disclosure of commercial or affiliate relationships. Where authorized, licensed gambling advertising must comply with statutory responsible gaming disclosure rules enforced by DC Lottery & Office of Lottery and Gaming (OLG).

General commercial advertising in Washington DC-Hagerstown is subject to truth-in-advertising standards under District of Columbia Consumer Protection Procedures Act (DC Code § 28-3901 et seq.).. Covered gambling promotions are separately subject to statutory responsible gaming disclosures under state gaming regulations (DC Lottery & Office of Lottery and Gaming (OLG)).

5. Sweepstakes, Social Casino & Prediction Markets

Promotional sweepstakes in DC are subject to the DC Consumer Protection Procedures Act (DC Code § 28-3901).

Dual-Currency & Simulated Gambling: Must adhere strictly to DC consumer protection and lottery definitions.
Statutory Code: DC Code § 28-3901
Prediction Markets: Event-contract prediction markets may fall under CFTC federal oversight, while state gambling and consumer-protection laws can also create separate legal or enforcement issues.

6. Municipal & DMA Local Context

Washington DC operates under the DC Consumer Protection Procedures Act (DC Code § 28-3901) enforced by the DC Attorney General and DLCP.
