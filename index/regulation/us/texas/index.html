State Legal Baseline Overview

In Texas, commercial advertising and consumer protection are governed generally by Texas Attorney General under Texas Deceptive Trade Practices-Consumer Protection Act (DTPA) (Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 17.41). Gambling activities and licensed promotional rules are separately regulated by Texas Lottery Commission. Commercial online real-money casinos and sports wagering are prohibited under Texas Penal Code § 47.01 et seq.

Consumer Protection & Enforcement

Consumer protection in Texas is enforced by the Texas Attorney General under Texas Deceptive Trade Practices-Consumer Protection Act (DTPA) (Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 17.41), requiring clear pricing disclosures, non-deceptive terms, and truthful marketing. Specific gambling disclosures are governed under Texas Lottery Commission regulations.
