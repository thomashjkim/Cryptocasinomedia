UNITED KINGDOM REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) Compliance Guide (2026)

The UK represents one of the global gold standards for iGaming regulation, enforcing strict player protection, financial risk assessments, and advertising compliance under UKGC oversight.

Primary Statutory Authority

Regulatory Body: UK Gambling Commission (UKGC)

Governing Legislation: Gambling Act 2005 & Government White Paper Reform Framework (2023)

Verify UKGC Official Site →

Cryptocurrency & Financial Risk Oversight

UKGC enforces stringent source-of-funds (SOF) and customer due diligence (CDD) standards for digital asset deposits prior to wagering.

UKGC LCCP provisions require verified source of funds (SOF) and customer due diligence before crypto asset deposit execution.

Advertising Codes & Consumer Disclosures (ASA / CAP)

ASA / CAP Code regulations enforce strict non-appeal to minors, clear promotional terms, and mandatory Take Time To Think / GambleAware disclaimers.
