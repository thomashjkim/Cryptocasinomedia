GERMANY REGULATORY GUIDE

Germany Crypto Gambling & iGaming Framework (2026)

Germany enforces a nationwide regulated iGaming regime under the Interstate Treaty on Gambling 2021 (GlüStV 2021) supervised by GGL in Halle.

Regulatory Authority & Governing Law

Primary Authority: Gemeinsame Glücksspielbehörde der Länder (GGL)

Statutory Baseline: Glücksspielstaatsvertrag 2021 (GlüStV 2021)

Verify Official Authority →

Cryptocurrency & Digital Asset Oversight

Cryptocurrency wagering is unauthorized; compliant operators are restricted to EUR fiat bank and e-wallet methods.

Advertising & Marketing Guidelines

Strict broadcasting time restrictions, prohibition of cross-sell marketing, and mandatory stake limit warnings.
