Executive Summary

SAN FRANCISCO, Oct. 01, 2026 — SAN FRANCISCO — Cross-chain payment telemetry compiled by CryptoCasinoMedia Research reveals that monthly wager and payout settlement volume on Solana Pay and Coinbase's Base Layer-2 network achieved an all-time high of $1.24 billion in September 2026, up 68% quarter-over-quarter.

The rapid surge underscores a structural transformation in online gaming payments. As high-volume digital casino and sportsbook operators phase out legacy proof-of-work settlement delays, high-speed networks featuring sub-second block finality and negligible network fees have become the new industry benchmark for real-time cashier systems.

According to the quarterly payment intelligence report, Solana-native settlements accounted for $710 million of total volume, fueled by widespread adoption of USDC-SPL and automated wallet connection standards. Meanwhile, Base processed $530 million, bolstered by frictionless onramps from Coinbase's retail ecosystem and native Smart Wallet integration eliminating seed-phrase friction for mainstream bettors.

“Players no longer tolerate 15-minute confirmations or variable network gas spikes when depositing or requesting cashouts,” said Elena Rostova, Lead Financial Infrastructure Analyst at CCM Research. “Solana and Base have transformed cashier economics. Average operator network overhead has plummeted by over 94%, enabling real-time instant cashouts directly to non-custodial wallets within 500 milliseconds of bet settlement.”

Major iGaming software suppliers and white-label platforms have adapted swiftly to the shift. Over 40 enterprise gaming platforms integrated turnkey Solana Pay and Base RPC nodes during Q3 2026, offering players zero-gas deposit promotions subsidized by platform smart-contract paymasters.

Security and settlement finality have matched the speed gains. Telemetry indicates zero network halts or reorg exploits across both networks throughout the reporting period. With Circle's Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol (CCTP) providing native capital fungibility, industry executives project Solana and Base will process over 45% of all global crypto iGaming settlement volume by mid-2027.
