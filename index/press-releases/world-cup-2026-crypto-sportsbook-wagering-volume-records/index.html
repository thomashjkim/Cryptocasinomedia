Executive Summary

Crypto sportsbooks report record-breaking transaction volumes as the group stage of the FIFA World Cup 2026 concludes.

LONDON, UK, Jun. 29, 2026 — The FIFA World Cup 2026 is setting new precedents not just on the pitch, but across the digital wagering landscape. According to a quarterly report released today by iGaming Market Intelligence, cryptocurrency sportsbooks have experienced an unprecedented 340% year-over-year surge in daily active users and bet volume, surpassing all initial industry projections.

Frictionless micro-betting options and low-cost stablecoin payouts are driving the increase, particularly in high-volume regions like Latin America and Southeast Asia. Traditional fiat bookmakers are seeing their margins squeezed by comparison due to slower banking rails and high currency conversion fees.

“We are witnessing a structural transition where users are treating their Web3 wallets as unified gaming and betting portals,” noted a chief market analyst. “The instant settlement times of USDT and USDC on layer-2 networks are proving to be the ultimate conversion tool for sports bettors who want immediate access to their winnings.”

This volume surge is also prompting leading platforms to introduce customized 'World Cup Series' rewards, offering players fractional ownership in Web3 betting pools and real-time cashbacks.
