ATLANTA, May. 22, 2024 /GlobeNewswire/ -- BitPay, a recognized technology leader in the interactive gaming and digital asset sectors, today unveiled its latest deployment of Cross-Border Real-Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) Gateway, establishing a new industry benchmark for frictionless fiat-to-crypto bridging across Latin American and Asian corridors.

The launch addresses growing operational demand across interactive wagering ecosystems, where operators require robust, high-availability transaction processing alongside uncompromising cryptographic verification. By integrating direct liquidity channels and automated settlement frameworks, the architecture reduces overhead while ensuring round-the-clock reliability.

"Our team is committed to developing infrastructure that bridges the rigorous requirements of global gaming regulators with the speed and transparency of next-generation clearing rails," said a spokesperson for BitPay. "This milestone demonstrates our dedication to providing institutional-grade technology that empowers both operators and end-users with unparalleled performance and safety."

As part of the deployment, comprehensive third-party audit reports and technical specifications have been finalized. The solution is fully operational across supported jurisdictions, with further cross-platform integrations scheduled throughout the upcoming fiscal quarters.
