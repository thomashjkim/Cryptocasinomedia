Executive Summary

Inflection Point Acquisition Corp. VI (Nasdaq: IPFX) ("Inflection Point"), a special purpose acquisition company led by the management team of...

NEW YORK /PRNewswire/ — Until the Closing, Inflection Point's ordinary shares and warrants will continue to trade on Nasdaq under the ticker symbols "IPFX" and "IPFXW," respectively. Inflection Point's units will continue to trade on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol "IPFXU" until the domestication of Inflection Point as a Delaware corporation prior to the Closing. The delisting from Nasdaq and the listings on the NYSE and NYSE American are each subject to the Closing and the satisfaction of all applicable listing requirements.

Upon the Closing, the combined company will be renamed "Quantum Space, Inc." (f/k/a IPFX PubCo, Inc.). Quantum Space's Class A-1 common stock is expected to begin trading on the NYSE under the ticker symbol "QSPC." Its warrants are expected to begin trading under the ticker symbol "QSPC WS" on NYSE American. The Business Combination is expected to close on November 23, 2026, and trading is expected to commence on or about November 24, 2026. In connection with the Closing and the expected listings on the NYSE and NYSE American, Nasdaq intends to halt trading of Inflection Point's securities after market close on November 20, 2026, and the last day of trading of Inflection Point's securities on Nasdaq is expected to be on or about that date.

As previously announced, Inflection Point has called an Extraordinary General Meeting (the "Extraordinary General Meeting") of its shareholders to be held at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time on November 20, 2026, at the offices of White & Case LLP, 1221 Avenue of the Americas, New York, NY 10020, and virtually via live webcast at https://www.cstproxy.com/inflectionpointacquisitionVI/2026. Shareholders of record of Inflection Point as of October 26, 2026 (the "Record Date") are entitled to vote on proposals to approve the Business Combination and the other proposals described in the definitive proxy statement/prospectus (the "Proxy Statement") included in the registration statement on Form S-4 (File No. 333-299286) (the "Registration Statement"), which the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the "SEC") declared effective on October 28, 2026. The Proxy Statement with respect to the Business Combination, together with a proxy card for voting, has been mailed to Inflection Point's shareholders. Final voting results will be disclosed in a Current Report on Form 8-K to be filed with the SEC following the Extraordinary General Meeting.

About Quantum Space‍Quantum Space is building the next generation of advanced maneuverable spacecraft for national security, civil, and commercial space operations. Its highly maneuverable spacecraft are being designed to address the needs of national security and commercial operators. With patented propulsion, extended on-orbit endurance, and modular flexibility, these platforms are being engineered to outmaneuver legacy satellites and operate dynamically across diverse mission sets. For more information, visit www.quantumspaceinc.com.

About Inflection Point Acquisition Corp. VI‍Inflection Point Acquisition Corp. VI is a special purpose acquisition company whose business purpose is to effect a merger, share exchange, asset acquisition, share purchase, reorganization, or similar business combination with one or more businesses or entities. Inflection Point is led by an experienced SPAC sponsor team that seeks to identify, take public, and scale high-impact and strategically important technology companies. The sponsor team's prior transactions include Intuitive Machines (Nasdaq: LUNR) and USA Rare Earth (Nasdaq: USAR).
