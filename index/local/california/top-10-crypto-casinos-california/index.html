Frequently Asked Questions: California Crypto Gaming & Regulations

Are crypto casinos legal in California?

California law does not authorize or license commercial online casinos. The California Gambling Control Commission (CGCC) regulates tribal gaming facilities and cardrooms, but does not grant online licenses. California residents who access crypto casino platforms interact with offshore operators licensed in jurisdictions like Curaçao and Anjouan.

How did California Assembly Bill 831 (AB 831) affect sweepstakes and crypto casinos?

Enacted in 2026, California Assembly Bill 831 strictly prohibited dual-currency promotional sweepstakes casino models (Gold Coins and Sweeps Coins). Consequently, domestic sweepstakes platforms face severe operational bans in California, causing players to migrate toward offshore cryptocurrency wagering platforms.

Do I have to pay taxes on crypto casino winnings in California?

Yes. Both the California Franchise Tax Board (FTB) and the IRS require all gambling winnings—including those paid in Bitcoin, Ethereum, or stablecoins—to be reported as taxable income at fair market value at the time of distribution (Form 1040 Schedule 1 and CA Form 540).

What payout speeds and settlement protocols do crypto casinos support in California?

Leading offshore crypto casinos settle withdrawals directly via on-chain cryptocurrency transactions. Bitcoin (BTC) typically requires 10-20 minutes, while Solana (SOL) and stablecoins like TRON-based USDT or Ethereum ERC-20 settle in under 5 minutes with automated instant payout thresholds.
