Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 30, 2024 (Last Updated: July 2026)

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how Moonoh Inc. ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit and use our APP and website https://moon-oh.com.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

We collect information to provide a safe and functional marketplace for buying and selling:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and optional phone number.
  • Device and Browsing Information: IP address, device type, operating system.
  • Specific Permissions: Camera, Photo Library, and Location permissions to enable product listing creation and neighborhood proximity search.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to operate the marketplace, facilitate local buyer-seller connections, maintain community security, and comply with legal requirements. We never sell your personal data to third-party data brokers.
