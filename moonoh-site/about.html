About Moonoh Inc.

Moonoh Inc. was incorporated in New York in 2021, and the moonoh local marketplace app officially launched in 2024. Designed specifically for local communities, Moonoh connects buyers and sellers across New York City and parts of the New Jersey area to facilitate safe, fee-free, local secondhand commerce.

Our Mission

We believe that buying and selling locally should be simple, safe, and community-oriented. By offering zero listing fees, secure in-app messaging, and local neighborhood meetup coordination, Moonoh helps neighbors declutter, discover unique pre-loved items, and build vibrant local connections.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any fees to buy or sell on Moonoh?

No. Moonoh is a 100% free local neighborhood marketplace. There are zero listing fees and zero selling commissions.

Where is Moonoh currently available?

Moonoh is exclusively designed for local communities across New York City (all five boroughs) and parts of the New Jersey area.

How are transactions handled?

Buyers and sellers connect via secure in-app messaging to arrange safe, in-person local meetups to exchange secondhand items.
