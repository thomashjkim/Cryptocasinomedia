Frequently Asked Questions

Is Moonoh really free to use?

Yes. Moonoh is 100% free to download and use. We never take a percentage of your sales or charge listing fees.

Where does Moonoh operate?

Moonoh operates across all 5 boroughs of New York City (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx, Staten Island) and neighboring areas in New Jersey (Jersey City, Hoboken, Fort Lee, Bergen County).

How do I meet up with buyers and sellers?

Once you agree on a price via Moonoh's in-app chat, arrange to meet in a well-lit, public location such as a local cafe, subway entrance, or apartment lobby during daylight hours.
