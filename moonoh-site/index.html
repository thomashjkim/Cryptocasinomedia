How moonoh works

1. Find Treasures or List Your Item

Discover a world of unique secondhand items just waiting to be found in your neighborhood, or list your pre-loved items for free in seconds.

2. Connect and Chat Securely

Chat instantly with sellers or buyers using built-in secure messaging. Ask questions, negotiate prices, and arrange meetups with local neighbors safely.

3. Complete the Exchange

Meet up at a mutually agreed local spot (coffee shop, public concourse, or police exchange zone). Hand over the item and complete your transaction.

4. Rate Your Experience

Mark the item as sold within the app. Both buyer and seller leave mutual ratings and reviews to build a trustworthy local community.
ABOUT MOONOH

moonoh (Moonoh Inc.) is a 100% free local marketplace mobile app serving residents across New York City and New Jersey. Founded in 2024 by CEO Ryan Seungmin Moon, moonoh enables neighbors to list secondhand items, chat securely in-app, coordinate safe local meetups, and exchange goods with zero listing fees and zero transaction commissions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is moonoh?

moonoh is a free local neighborhood marketplace mobile app designed for New York City and New Jersey residents to buy and sell secondhand items directly with verified neighbors.

How much does it cost to use moonoh?

moonoh is 100% free to download and use on both iOS and Android. There are zero listing fees, zero selling fees, and zero buyer transaction commissions.

Where does moonoh operate?

moonoh currently operates across all five boroughs of New York City (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, Staten Island) and select communities in New Jersey.

How is moonoh different from Facebook Marketplace or OfferUp?

Unlike nation-wide platforms, moonoh is hyper-localized for NYC and NJ communities, featuring 0% transaction fees, mandatory local in-person exchanges, built-in secure messaging, and a mutual community trust rating system.
