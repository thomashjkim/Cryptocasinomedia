Fraud Prevention & Safety Guidelines

Moonoh is dedicated to maintaining a safe, transparent neighborhood marketplace. Follow these safety practices when exchanging items in New York City or New Jersey:

1. Meet in Well-Lit, Public Locations: Always agree to exchange items during daylight hours in busy public spaces such as coffee shops, police station exchange zones, or subway station concourses.
2. Keep Communication In-App: Use Moonoh's secure in-app messaging to discuss price and meetup logistics. Do not share personal financial details or home addresses.
3. Inspect Items Before Completing Payment: Verify the condition and authenticity of pre-loved items in person prior to concluding the transaction.
4. Report Suspicious Activity: Report fraudulent listings, counterfeit items, or abusive users immediately via the in-app reporting button or by emailing info@moon-oh.com.
